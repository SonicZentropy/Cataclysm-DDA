# Bring Out Your Dead Mod Summary

## Overview

Bring Out Your Dead is a Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead mod that adds futuristic technology, bionics, robotic enemies, and high-tech locations to the game. 

## Key Features

### Bionics (CBMs)

The mod introduces numerous cyberbiotic modules (CBMs) that players can install:

- **Railgun**: EM field generators that increase thrown object damage and range
- **Trickle Charger**: Passive power regeneration CBM
- **Power Armor Interface**: Allows drawing power from bionics to power armor
- **Solar Panels**: Retractable panels that recharge in sunlight
- **Microfusion Generator**: Long-lasting internal fusion reactor
- **Deployable Grenade Launcher**: Wrist-mounted 40mm grenade launcher
- **Linguistic Coprocessor**: Increases reading speed
- **Kinetic Shock Absorbers**: Reduces fall damage at cost of movement
- **Dopamine Stimulators**: Induces euphoria and suppresses fear
- **Cable Charger System**: Recharges bionics when connected to power sources

These bionics are organized into various item groups for different factions and locations (military, scientific, elite operators, etc.).

### Furniture

- **Autodoc Mk. XI**: Surgical apparatus for bionic installation/removal (requires programming)
- **Autodoc operation couch**: Plush red sofa for autodoc operations

### Harvest & Dissection

Special harvest actions allow players to recover bionics from specific zombie types:

- Zombie scientists yield bionic power storage and science bionics
- Zombie technicians yield bionic power storage and technician bionics
- Military zombies yield military bionics
- Bio-operators yield elite bio-operator bionics (offense, defense, utility)

### Techniques

- **Pressure Crunch**: Unarmed/melee technique that deals 1.5x bash damage with stun and knockback (crit only)

### Vehicles

Atomic-powered vehicles featuring mini-reactors:

- Atomic Compact: Small efficient car
- Atomic SUV: Larger family vehicle
- Atomic Car: Standard sedan
- Bubble Car: Futuristic enclosed vehicle
- Atomic Sports Car: High-performance variant

### Monsters

New robotic and zombie enemies:

- **Robots**: Eyebots (scouting), Copbots (law enforcement), Riotbots (crowd control), Chickenbots (heavy combat walkers)
- **Feral Humans**: Feral soldiers with flamethrowers
- **Zombie Variants**:
  - Electric zombies (shockers) with ranged lightning attacks
  - Lab zombies (scientists, technicians) that yield bionics when dissected
  - Soldier zombies with black-ops camouflage and bio-operator variants
  - Winged electric raptors

### Recipes

New crafting recipes include:

- Control Laptop: For commanding robots via ultra-high frequencies
- Acid Bomb: Throwable acid container
- Superglue: Bone-based adhesive
- Robot Recipes: Build eyebots, copbots, and riotbots from scavenged parts
- Wire Crafting: Create baskets and folding baskets from wire
- Construction: Place autodocs and autodoc couches

### Map Generation

New structures and locations:

- **Augmentation Clinic**: Medical facility for bionic installation (multiple floors)
- **Bank**: Vaulted building with teller stations and security systems
- **Basement Bionic**: Residential basement with autodoc
- **Military Base (Z0)**: Large military complex with barracks, armory, hospital, and vehicle bays
- **Lab Facilities**: Vehicle testing tracks and weapons ranges
- **Map Extras**: XEDRA trucks carrying supplies and scientists

### Items

Extensive new item additions:

- **Batteries**: Various atomic and ultra-light battery types with different capacities
- **Electronics**: Atomic smartphones (with flashlight/music modes), powerbanks (UPS)
- **Tools**: Atompot (self-heating kitchen appliance), bionic power port mod
- **Weapons**: Futuristic firearms (FN FSC2000, Colt Lightning), energy swords, titanium bats, deployed grenade launchers, alien plasma bows, hydraulic gauntlets
- **Books**: Martial arts manuals (Centipede, Lizard, Scorpion, Toad, Viper Kung Fu) and robot schematics
- **Vehicle Parts**: Mini-reactors, helicopter rotors
- **Corpses**: Inactive bot remains for scavenging

### Item Groups

Numerous item groups for loot distribution, including:

- Bionics tiers (common, military, scientific, operator variants)
- Robot components
- Military supplies and gear
- SWAT equipment
- Various crafting material groups

### Overmap & Regional Overlay

- New overmap terrains for augmentation clinics and basement bionics
- Regional settings that add augmentation clinics to city shops
- Map extras like XEDRA trucks on roads

### Migration

Updates entity IDs from older versions (e.g., `bot_eyebot` → `afs_bot_eyebot`)

### Construction Groups

- Place Autodoc Mk. XI
- Place Autodoc operation couch
