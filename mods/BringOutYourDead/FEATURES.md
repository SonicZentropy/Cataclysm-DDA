# Bring Out Your Dead Mod - Master List of Changes

## Vehicles
### New Vehicles
- **truck_XEDRA**: "Trans-Coast Logistics Truck" - A large armored truck with reinforced plating, diesel engine, multiple seats, storage areas, and equipped with SWAT gear, weapons, mutagens, and medical supplies.
- **compact_atomic**: "Atomic Compact" - A small electric vehicle with minireactor power source, seats, controls, and basic storage.
- **suv_atomic**: "Atomic SUV" - A mid-size electric SUV with minireactor, multiple seats, storage, and items like car kits and snacks.
- **car_atomic**: "Atomic Car" - A standard electric car with minireactor, seats, and storage for kits and food.
- **bubble_car**: "Bubble Car" - A unique vehicle with electric engine, minireactor, multiple seats, and equipped with car kits.
- **car_sports_atomic**: "Atomic Sports Car" - A high-performance electric sports car with enhanced electric engine, slick wheels, leather seats, and luxury items.
